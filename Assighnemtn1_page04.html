A Simple Daily Routine

The following Is an example of a suggested time table that could generally work for anyone. If it doesn’t work according to your needs, at least the template is useful.

A good daily routine can help students balance classes, study, travel, meals and free time. Planning the day in advance can also make it easier to stay organised and avoid wasting long breaks between classes.

Suggested One-Day Schedule

Time Activity Location Tip
8:00 AM Prepare and have breakfast Home Check your timetable before leaving
9:00 AM Travel to university Bayswater to Hawthorn Allow extra time for traffic or delays
10:00 AM Attend first class Swinburne Hawthorn Arrive a few minutes early
12:00 PM Lunch Campus or Glenferrie Road Bringing lunch from home can save money
1:00 PM Study or work on assignments Library Use gaps between classes productively
2:30 PM Attend afternoon class Swinburne Hawthorn Take notes and ask questions when needed
4:30 PM Review the day's work Study area Write down anything you need to revise
5:00 PM Travel home Hawthorn Check traffic or public transport before leaving
7:00 PM Dinner and break Home Take some time away from study
8:00 PM Short study session Home Prepare for the next day

Why This Schedule Works

A schedule like this gives students enough time for classes, independent study, meals, travel and rest.

This is not the time table that I follow as I have made it for generally and anyone could customize it in their own preferred way.

Planning your day does not mean every minute has to be fixed. The main goal is to have a simple routine that helps you stay organised and manage your workload more easily.
