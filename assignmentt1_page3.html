Place That I Recommend

Even though I’m new to this campus, at the moment I would recommend the place that I spend the most time in, which is the Late Lab.

Here, there is plenty of space to be in your comfort zone and study or hang out, either by yourself or with your friends. It is also a place to have your food, as on the third floor there are microwaves. There is also a small café where you can have coffee or a quick snack. Multiple vending machines are also available.

My Experience

Since the start of my course, I thought having a good place to study is important. One reason I chose the Late Lab is that it is open 24 hours. On the other hand, it is opposite the ATC building, in which most of my classes are.

It is peaceful, it is quiet and it is comfortable here at the Late Lab.

Why I Recommend It

The Late Lab is a place where you can get many things done, from studying to having your food. It can also help students manage their time better, especially if they travel a long distance to university and do not want to go home between classes.
