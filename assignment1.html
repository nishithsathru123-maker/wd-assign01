My Experience at Swinburne

A turning point of my life

Making my way to Swinburne University of Technology became one turning point of my life as an international student. The first few days on campus are where you learn about where to go, what to ask and when to do things. One of the most important things is meeting new friends. I met several people from my own country as well as other countries, and it is a pleasure to learn about different cultures and ethics.

One of the biggest differences in university compared to other educational stages of your life is that you are more independent. The lecturers guide you through the content of the study, and it is your responsibility to keep up, complete assignments and manage your time.

University might be confusing at first, but it becomes much easier once you become familiar with the campus and develop a good routine.

3 Things I Wish I Knew

  • Explore the Campus Early

    After coming to Australia from Sri Lanka, I had around 2 weeks before campus started. Therefore, I had plenty of time to get familiar with the country and, most importantly, explore the campus. But I waited until the last minute to explore the campus, so don't make that mistake yourself. Knowing your classrooms, library, study areas and food places can make your first week much less stressful.

  • Plan Your Transport

    According to how I felt, Glenferrie Station is kind of a central hub where a lot of train lines are connected. If you are planning to use public transportation, trains are by far the most effective. Get your myki card as early as possible and plan your way to campus.

    If you are planning to drive to campus, which I have now transitioned to, be concerned about parking. It is important for you to have a digital parking permit which offers lower rates without time restrictions. Before driving to campus, have a walk the day before and see the places where you can actually park.

  • Start Assignments Early

    Assignments can take longer than you expect. Starting early gives you enough time to understand the task, ask your tutor questions and correct mistakes before submitting your work.

My Advice for Students

The main thing is to manage your time. You could most probably be doing part-time work alongside studying. Make sure to attend classes and start assignments on time before things start getting stressful.

You should keep in mind that your main goal is to complete your course with high achievements. At the same time, it is crucial to have some rest between study and work, avoiding brain fatigue and mental health problems.
