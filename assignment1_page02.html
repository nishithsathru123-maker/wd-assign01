3 Things I Wish I Knew

  • Explore the Campus Early

    After coming to Australia from Sri Lanka, I had around 2 weeks before campus started. Therefore I had plenty of time to get familiar with the country and most importantly, explore the campus. But I had to wait until the last minute to explore the campus so don't make that mistake for yourself. Knowing your classrooms, library, study areas and food places can make your first week much less stressful.

  • Plan Your Transport

    According to how I felt, Glenferrie Station is kind of a central hub where a lot of train lines are connected. If you are planning to use public transportation, trains are by far the most effective. Get your myki card as early as possible and plan your way to campus.

    If you are planning to drive to campus, which now I have transitioned to, be concerned about parking. It is important for you to have a digital parking permit which offers less rates without time restrictions. Before driving to campus, have a walk the day before and see the places where you can actually park.

  • Start Assignments Early

    Assignments can take longer than you expect. Starting early gives you enough time to understand the task, ask your tutor questions and correct mistakes before submitting your work.

My Advice for Students

The main thing is manage your time. You could most probably be doing part time work alongside studying. Make sure to attend classes and start assignments on time before things start getting stressful. You should keep in mind that your main goal is to complete your course with high achievements.

At the same time, it is crucial to have some rest between study and work, avoiding brain fatigue and mental health diseases.
